# NEXARIS — Retail Food Brand & Global Export House Upgrade

Transforming **NEXARIS** (`nexaris.co.in`) into an integrated **Consumer Retail Brand & Global Agricultural Export House** encompassing **Spices**, **Dal & Pulses**, and **Dry Fruits**, strictly structured in the visual architecture of the provided reference design while executed in NEXARIS's institutional palette: **Deep Navy** (`#0A192F` / `#0F172A`), **Warm Gold** (`#D4AF37` / `#C5A059`), and **Warm Ivory** (`#FAF8F5`).

---

## User Review & Critical Decisions

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Strategic Business Pivot Confirmed by User**:
> - **Jaggery De-emphasized/Replaced**: Transitioned from a pure jaggery focus to 3 core consumer & export commodity divisions:
>   1. **Wholesale & Retail Spices**: Cumin, Fennel, Coriander, Mustard, Sesame, Ajwain, Turmeric Powder, Red Chilli Powder, and Dhana-Jeera Powder.
>   2. **Dal & Pulses**: Chana Dal, Toor Dal, Moong Dal (Whole & Split), Urad Dal, Masoor Dal, and Kabuli Chana.
>   3. **Dry Fruits & Nuts**: Premium California Almonds (Badam), Whole Cashews (Kaju), Pistachios (Pista), Seedless Raisins (Kismis), and Kashmiri Walnuts (Akhrot).
> - **Dual Posture**: NEXARIS is positioned as a **Consumer Retail Brand** (stand-up retail pouches: 100g, 250g, 500g, 1kg) with a dedicated **Global Export Division** (20ft/40ft FCL bulk container dispatch via Mundra & Kandla ports).

---

## 1. Overview & Core Concept

- **What It Does**: Presents NEXARIS as an established Indian agro-processing and retail food brand, offering end-to-end processing, Sortex cleaning, and consumer retail packaging alongside international container exports.
- **Target Audience**: Domestic retail distributors, supermarket procurement managers, HORECA buyers, and international commodity importers across the GCC, UK/Europe, and Southeast Asia.
- **Key Value**: Farm-gate origin procurement in Gujarat and Western India, certified clean-room packaging, unadulterated food safety (FSSAI, APEDA, Spices Board, FDA, Halal), and FOB/CIF trade execution.

### Visual Identity & Theme
- **Dominant Palette**:
  - **Canvas & Backdrops**: Deep Navy (`#0A192F` / `#0F172A`) for header, hero overlay, metrics, and quote banner; Warm Ivory (`#FAF8F5`) for content sections.
  - **Primary Accents**: Warm Gold (`#D4AF37` / `#C5A059`) for headers, two-tone titles (`KEY <span className="text-[#D4AF37]">PROCESS</span>`), active tabs, and primary buttons.
  - **Typography**: `Newsreader` / `Playfair Display` serif for high-authority headings paired with `Plus Jakarta Sans` for clean body readability and `JetBrains Mono` for tabular metrics and pack sizes.
- **Anti-Slop Discipline**:
  - No synthetic CSS dot grids or generic gradients.
  - Clean photographic imagery of real Indian spices, lentils, and dry fruits.
  - Authentic, verifiable metrics and certified factory capabilities.

## 3. Product Catalogue Restructure

The product catalogue will be organized into **3 Core Consumer & Export Divisions**:

### Division 1: Wholesale & Retail Spices
- **Whole Spices**: Premium Cumin Seeds (Jeera), Fennel Seeds (Variyali), Coriander Seeds (Dhana), Mustard Seeds (Rai), Natural & Hulled Sesame Seeds (Til), Ajwain Seeds (Carom).
- **Ground Spice Powders**: Red Chilli Powder (Guntur & Kashmiri stemless), Turmeric Powder (Curcumin 3%–5%), Coriander-Cumin Blend (Dhana-Jeera Powder).
- **Packaging Sizes**: 100g, 250g, 500g, 1kg retail pouches; 25kg / 50kg bulk export bags.

### Division 2: Dal, Lentils & Pulses
- **Varieties**: Toor Dal (Oiled & Unpolished Pigeon Pea), Chana Dal (Split Desi Chickpeas), Moong Dal (Yellow Split & Green Whole), Urad Dal (Black Gram Split & Whole), Masoor Dal (Split Red Lentils), Kabuli Chana (Dollar Grade Chickpeas).
- **Packaging Sizes**: 500g, 1kg, 5kg retail packs; 25kg, 50kg woven PP bags for institutional & export supply.

### Division 3: Dry Fruits & Nuts
- **Varieties**: California Whole Almonds (Badam), Whole Cashews (Kaju Grade W240/W320), Iranian Pistachios (Pista Salted/Roasted), Golden Long Raisins (Kismis), Kashmiri Walnut Kernels (Akhrot).
- **Packaging Sizes**: 200g, 250g, 500g, 1kg food-grade vacuum pouches; 10kg master cartons.
